## Business Problem Ownership

The dashboard addresses five business problems. To represent the team's contribution clearly, primary Power BI/business-analysis ownership is divided as follows.

### Harshith Katta — 2 Business Problems

#### 1. Profitability Analysis
Primary focus:
- Category profitability
- Product profitability
- Channel / Region / Marketing performance
- Delivered Orders, Net Sales, Total Profit, and Profit Margin validation

#### 2. Fulfilment & Revenue Risk
Primary focus:
- At-Risk Net Sales
- At-Risk Sales Share
- Order-status exposure
- Regional/status risk concentration

In addition to these two dashboard problems, Harshith's largest overall contribution was the preprocessing and data-quality workflow that prepared trusted Silver data for all five business problems.

### Keerthi Priya — 3 Business Problems

#### 1. Discount Impact on Profitability
Primary focus:
- Discount-band analysis

#### 2. Customer Segment Performance
Primary focus:
- Profitability by Customer Segment
- Customer satisfaction by segment
- Interpretation of segment behaviour and value

#### 3. Customer Experience vs Fulfilment
Primary focus:
- Customer satisfaction by Order Status
- Delivery Rating comparison

- Visual presentation of fulfilment and customer-experience relationships

Keerthi Priya also took a stronger role in the Power BI presentation layer, including visual selection, page composition, and communicating business insights from the Gold views.
